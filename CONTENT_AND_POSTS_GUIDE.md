## 🚀 How to Schedule 2–3 Posts a Day Automatically (100% Free & Safe)

> **⚠️ CRITICAL SECURITY WARNING:**
> Never share your social media passwords with automated third-party bots or AI scripts. Instagram and TikTok have strict fraud prevention systems (2FA and device fingerprinting). Automated unauthorized logins will lock or ban your accounts!

### Option A: Official Meta Business Suite (Best for Instagram & Facebook)
1. Go to **[business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com)** on your computer.
2. Link your Instagram account (`@binirfanfragrances`) to your Facebook page.
3. Click on **Planner** in the left menu.
4. Click **Create Post** or **Create Reel**.
5. Select both Instagram and Facebook.
6. Upload your video or picture, paste the captions and hashtags provided above.
7. Click the dropdown next to Publish and select **Schedule**.
8. Set the date and time (e.g. 1:30 PM, 6:30 PM, 9:30 PM).
9. You can schedule up to **75 days of content in advance** in under 30 minutes!

### Option B: TikTok Web Desktop Scheduler (Best for TikTok)
1. Open your PC browser and go to **[tiktok.com/creator-center](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-center)** or **tiktok.com/upload**.
2. Log into your TikTok account.
3. Drag & drop your video.
4. Paste the caption & hashtags.
5. Toggle on **Schedule video**.
6. Select your preferred date and time (up to 10 days in advance).
7. Click **Schedule**!

### Option C: Metricool / Buffer (All-in-One Free Tool)
- If you want a single dashboard that posts to **Instagram Reels and TikTok at the exact same time**:
  - Sign up for **Metricool.com** (free tier supports 1 brand, 50 scheduled posts/month).
  - Connect Instagram & TikTok with official OAuth login.
  - Drag and drop your posts into the visual calendar.
